# Review 1: evaluation methodology

Reviewer notes (internal, week of 2026-07-13).

Summary: This review covers evaluation methodology across the recent literature. The main
strengths are careful operationalization and reproducible pipelines. The main
weaknesses are small sample sizes and a reliance on single-model results.

Key points:
- Construct validity is under-argued in most cited works.
- Effect sizes are rarely reported with confidence intervals.
- Recommendation: replicate the two headline results before citing them.
